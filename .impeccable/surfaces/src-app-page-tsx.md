# Home (/) — and the shared system for /browse, /p/[id], /me

Mode: Persuade (home). Browse/post/me are Operate surfaces inheriting this world.
Audience: NCR renters arriving from WhatsApp/Facebook groups, mostly on phones. Action: search posts by city, kind and rent (working form in the first viewport).

## Direction contract

THESIS: NCR is a Metro network and every area is a station. The page refuses the headline-plus-three-cards template; the map itself is the hero and the doors into browse.

OWN-WORLD: white map ground, near-black ink, each city owns a line colour (Gurugram yellow, Delhi magenta, Noida blue, Ghaziabad green, Faridabad violet). Station dots with white centres, interchange rings for shared habits. Platform boards: near-black panels with amber numerals. Signage sans (Overpass), tabular numerals. Pills, not boxes.

STORY: see NCR as a network, pick your station, understand the match score as two lines meeting at interchanges, browse.

FIRST VIEWPORT: left 5/12: headline, one line of copy, working search (need/have toggle, city, max rent, Search). Right 7/12: schematic NCR map, five coloured lines with area stations; stations with live posts show counts; every station links to browse.

FORM: Delhi Metro network map (my list #1, IMPECCABLE'S PICK, chosen by user). seed:98a1c5e966181bfd

SIGNATURE: lines draw along their paths once on load; a train dot rides the yellow line; match breakdown on posts drawn as two lines meeting at interchange rings.
